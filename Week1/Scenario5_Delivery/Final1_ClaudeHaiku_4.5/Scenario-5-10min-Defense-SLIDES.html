Closing: Three Core Principles
1. Fail Closed, Not Open
Missing data? Escalate. Integration timeout? Escalate. Ambiguous rule? Escalate. Never guess or assume success.
2. Automate Mechanical, Escalate Judgment
Agent is a router and flagging system, not a clinician. Every escalation includes full context for human decision. Zero clinical judgment anywhere.
3. Audit Everything
Every action logged with actor, timestamp, details. Logs immutable. If there's a question later, the audit trail answers it.
This isn't just a spec. It's a safety boundary written into code.
The agent does what's mechanical, escalates what's clinical,
