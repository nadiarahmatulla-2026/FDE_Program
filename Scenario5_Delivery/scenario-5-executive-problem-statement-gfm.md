# Scenario 5 - Executive Problem Statement

## Problem Statement

A 6-physician family medicine practice operating across 2 locations sees approximately **180 patients per day**, but its **4-person front-desk team** is struggling to complete a complex patient intake workflow consistently. Required administrative tasks such as insurance verification, prior-authorisation checks, questionnaire collection, medication update collection, allergy-flag surfacing, and visit-reason routing are often incomplete by the start of the appointment. The most common failures are **expired prior authorisations** and **unreviewed medication changes**, which create delays, rework, and operational inefficiency.

The clinic needs an agentic workflow that improves intake completeness and reduces front-desk burden while staying strictly within **non-clinical administrative boundaries**. Any interpretation of symptoms, urgency, medication significance, allergy risk, or other **clinical judgment must remain with human staff**.

## Success Metrics (These are Assumed pending Stakeholder Verification)

| Metric | Target |
|:--|:--|
| Intake completeness rate | **>=95%** of visits begin with all required administrative intake steps completed or correctly escalated |
| Missed intake defects at visit start | **>=50% reduction** in missing prior-auth or medication-change issues discovered by physicians |
| Front-desk administrative time per patient | **30-40% reduction** |
| Same-day appointment disruption due to intake failures | **>=40% reduction** |
| Clinical-boundary violations by agent | **0** |
| Human escalation for potentially urgent or ambiguous visit reasons | **100%** routed for human review |
| Insurance and prior-auth status capture accuracy | **>=98%** |
| Audit logging for PHI, handoffs, and workflow actions | **100%** logged |
| Staff satisfaction and usability | **>=80% positive feedback** |
